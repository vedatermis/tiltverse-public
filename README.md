# Tiltverse (English)

**Tilt to steer the orb. 50 levels.**

For forty years Ganymede Station stood at the edge of the Ash Belt. Then one night the belt pulled the shore in. Only one thing still works: the backup power core. You.

Tiltverse is a balance and reflex game built entirely on tilt control. You never touch the screen — the orb moves exactly the way you hold the phone.

## What's in it

- **50 levels** across seven regions: the Wreck, the Deep, the Tower, the Last Light, the Belt, the Deep Belt, the Threshold
- Moving platforms, spinning arms, laser gates, collapsing floors
- Boosters, bumpers, force fields, teleport gates, pressure plates
- Energy orbs to collect in every level
- Bronze, silver and gold medals based on your time
- Checkpoints — a mistake doesn't send you back to the start
- Game Center leaderboards
- Plays with one hand, no internet required
